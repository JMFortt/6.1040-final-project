# Rubric: project management

Original: https://61040.github.io/fa26/rubrics/project-management/

This rubric covers only the slice of project management the final project asks for. It is not a general management textbook.

## What is required

- **Engagement.** Every teammate attends mentor meetings and takes part in respectful discussion with the team and the mentor.
- **Planning.** The plan is thoughtful and realistic about likely risks and about how large tasks are. Roles are explicit and fair. When a deadline is missed, the plan changes.
- **Agendas.** Each mentor meeting, and ideally each team meeting, has an agenda shared beforehand: topics and how long each gets.
- **Tracking.** Everyone reports progress and difficulties so the whole team knows what remains and what is blocked.

## Pitfalls

Some people dominate and others barely participate. Someone misses a mentor meeting and does not say so in advance. The team arrives without an agenda. Milestones are too vague to tell whether they happened.

## Levels

- **Deficient** — individuals barely working together
- **Emergent** — people are collaborating and writing plans and agendas, but those are often too vague to act on
- **Competent** — plan and agendas are concrete enough to guide the work; when the plan fails, the team records the issue and the decision and adjusts the plan
- **Expert** — the team also sees problems coming, and discusses options candidly when it addresses them

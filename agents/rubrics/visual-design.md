# Rubric: visual design

Original: https://61040.github.io/fa26/rubrics/visual-design/

Use this on a visual study and, later, on the running UI. Say which one you are looking at. Tutorial chrome left in a starter app is not a designed interface.

## Qualities

Organized grouping. Selective emphasis. Readable: contrast, size, line length. Consistent type, color, and space. Appearance that fits the purpose of this app.

## Pitfalls

Everything emphasized. Accidental grouping. Decoration with no purpose. A pile of components that do not belong together.

## Levels

- **Deficient** — hard to read or to parse
- **Emergent** — readable, weak hierarchy
- **Competent** — coherent and conventional, with some unresolved details
- **Expert** — overall structure and detail are both easy to grasp, and the character fits the app

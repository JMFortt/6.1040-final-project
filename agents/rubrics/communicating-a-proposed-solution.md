# Rubric: communicating a proposed solution

Original: https://61040.github.io/fa26/rubrics/communicating-a-proposed-solution/

Read the pitch, the sketches, and the user journey together, and check that they describe the same solution as the concept design.

## Qualities

- **Motivation** — a lay reader understands what the app does and why it helps the framed problem
- **Concrete interaction** — sketches show the essential elements and the transitions. Low fidelity is fine
- **Coherence** — pitch, sketches, concepts, and journey are one solution. The journey ties a person’s situation to actions and an outcome

## Pitfalls

A feature list with no case. Sketches that can be read several ways. A click-path with no person in it.

## Levels

- **Deficient** — no understandable solution, or no way to see how to use it
- **Emergent** — the idea is there; pitch, sketches, and journey gap or contradict each other
- **Competent** — clear, consistent essential interactions that address the problem
- **Expert** — a design choice that makes the person’s situation click, without marketing language

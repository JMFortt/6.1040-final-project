# Rubric: technical writing

Original: https://61040.github.io/fa26/rubrics/technical-writing/

Applies to design write-ups, specs, and the README. A design note may be less formal than an engine spec. Judge clarity either way.

## Qualities

Directed at a known reader. Consistent tone. Professional. Informative, so the reader does not have to join the dots. Precise. Self-contained. Clear and simple. Concise. Easy to scan: headings, short sentences, titled bullets. Accessible: an example or a diagram when the structure is visual.

## Pitfalls

- Formulaic padding, including “not merely X but Y,” and words that claim smoothness without saying what happens
- Skipping the part the writer does not understand
- Quantity over quality

## Levels

- **Deficient** — unstructured; unclear what it is saying
- **Emergent** — some structure and some points, no coherent whole
- **Competent** — the argument lands; it may be verbose, short on diagrams, or flat
- **Expert** — engaging, explanations a reader can reuse, personality without losing precision

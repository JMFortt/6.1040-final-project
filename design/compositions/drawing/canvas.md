# Persistent canvas

The team selected one persistent private canvas per artist. The [history endpoint](reaction:drawing.canvas.History) uses Sessioning.use to resolve the owner, then reads TimeLapsing._history. The [stroke endpoint](reaction:drawing.canvas.Stroke) resolves the owner the same way, then records a validated change with TimeLapsing.addUpdate. Request bodies cannot choose an owner. Empty histories represent blank canvases; no separate creation lifecycle is needed.

```endpoints
drawing.canvas.History at /canvas/history
drawing.canvas.Stroke at /canvas/stroke
drawing.canvas.Undo at /canvas/undo
drawing.canvas.Redo at /canvas/redo
drawing.canvas.Submit at /canvas/submit
drawing.canvas.Picture at /canvas/picture
drawing.canvas.Timelapses at /canvas/timelapses
drawing.canvas.Timelapse at /canvas/timelapse
```

Stroke values use renderer version `easy-brush-0.5.0-v1`, a fixed 1200 × 900 coordinate space, an ink, dry-brush, graphite, or eraser preset, hex color, size, opacity, and ordered points (x, y, pressure, elapsed milliseconds). The boundary validates every field, finite ranges, and monotonically increasing point times. A stroke contains at most 4096 points and is at most 524288 characters; long gestures continue in a new stroke. The record's timestamp captures the gesture's start time. Eraser updates remain in the history and replay with destination-out compositing. Textures are deterministic, versioned brush tips with no random jitter.

The browser queues completed strokes, sends them sequentially, and retains pending strokes for retry. UUID keys make retry after a lost response safe. A stale sequence triggers retrieval of new updates before retrying. An INVALID_REQUEST refusal stops retries and drawing while retaining the rejected stroke. The artist may explicitly discard that stroke; the browser then reconstructs the remaining drawing and resumes saving. No rejected stroke is silently discarded. History is paginated, so reload does not request the entire history in one response. The canvas blocks drawing until restoration completes and exposes save failures. In-app navigation waits for drawing and saving to finish. Leaving with unsaved strokes prompts the browser's normal unsaved-work warning.

[Undo](reaction:drawing.canvas.Undo) and [Redo](reaction:drawing.canvas.Redo) resolve the owner through Sessioning.use, then call TimeLapsing.undo and TimeLapsing.redo. Each takes a new UUID key, the next sequence, and a timestamp. Undo returns the sequence and the key of the stroke that left the drawing. Redo returns the sequence and that stroke's key, change, and occurrence time. The browser calls them only after the current gesture and pending strokes have saved. Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Y redoes. A new stroke after an undo discards the redo. History responses include the tip, the latest event sequence, and how many strokes can be redone. Restoration draws only the strokes still on the drawing.

[Submit](reaction:drawing.canvas.Submit) resolves the owner through Sessioning.use and calls TimeLapsing.complete with a UUID key, the expected next event sequence, and a WebP picture. The picture is the full 1200 × 900 render of the drawing, encoded in the browser from that raster and composited over white. It is not a capture of the screen or of the zoomed view. It is available after all strokes have saved and the drawing still has a stroke. Successful submission preserves that history and its picture as the next numbered timelapse and clears the working canvas; failure keeps the drawing visible. Retries reuse the same completion key, sequence, and picture bytes. The [picture endpoint](reaction:drawing.canvas.Picture) returns that stored WebP for one of the signed-in artist's recordings. A recording completed before pictures were stored answers found false. History responses include start, the latest completion's sequence, and the tip, so restoration shows only the current drawing. If another tab completes a history with local pending strokes, those strokes remain locally pending and are not silently moved into the next recording.

The authenticated [Timelapses](reaction:drawing.canvas.Timelapses) and [Timelapse](reaction:drawing.canvas.Timelapse) endpoints query TimeLapsing._recordings and _recording respectively. Neither accepts an owner from the client. The temporary private gallery lists submissions by ascending index, with paginated metadata and stroke loading. Each item shows the stored picture on its right when that recording has one. Recordings completed before pictures were stored have no thumbnail. Selecting one previews its final state and supports play, pause, resume, and replay with adjustable speed. All original points replay in sequence, including erasures; long pauses are compressed for viewing. Reference-image association, video export, and sharing remain deferred.

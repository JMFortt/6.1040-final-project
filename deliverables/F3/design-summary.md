# Design Summary
Our overall design goal is to create an app that helpers artists practice consistently by providing them with structure and social motivation.

The core concept that accomplishes both goals is ReferencePhotoBinning. This concept gives users reference photos to draw from (in a limited amount of time and within the website editor), but crucially separates references into fundamental skill categories (e.g. perspective, color theory) and gives all users active on the same day the same reference photos. Separating photos into fundamental categories provides structure while the limited bank provides motivation by reducing the burden of choice and building a community through shared struggle.

StreakTracking and CoverageTracking are additional tools to help users structure and motivate their practice. We hope that StreakTracking will provide artists with a small amount of pressure to practice regularly, while CoverageTracking will encourage structure by making the weaknesses and strengths of artists easier to visualize.

TimeLapsing augments the learning process by allowing users to see how other artists drew the same reference photos as them. This feature also addresses our social ethical concerns that artists may feel inadequate compared to others. By allowing them to see how other artists created an artwork from scratch, we hope that it makes the entire process less intimidating and more approachable.

We are currently unclear on our AI policy. We think it might be unfeasible or unscalable for us to perpetually add images to the bank. However, we are unsure if using AI for image collection/tagging for this will make artists uncomfortable using our app. Our plan is to avoid AI for our first checkpoint, then decide later on when we have a clearer picture of the full app whether we can continue without AI.

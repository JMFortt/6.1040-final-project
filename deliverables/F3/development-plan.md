## Development Plan
| Checkpoint name | Deliverable, risks, and fallbacks | Assignments
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design draft | Our first goal is to refine our concept design from this problem framing based on feedback from our graders and peers. At this stage, the concepts should be in the sync engine format and be ready to interface with the server. The primary risk is that some implementation detail later on may require us to refine our concept design. We plan to manage this risk by completing the MVP before the deadline to give us time to revise. | StreakTracking: Julia, TimeLapsing: Aden, CoverageTracking: Amina, ReferencePhotoBinning: Eric, Reactions: All of us |   
| Minimum viable product | Our second goal is a minimum implementation that includes key features/concepts. This includes the class reference bank, personal growth stats (fundamental spread and streak tracking), and drawing. At this stage, the focus will be the back-end and sync engine code. Our front-end will be minimal. The drawing interface will also be minimal, since it is mostly unspecified by the concept design and unconstrained by our server setup. Because this is our first implementation step, there is a risk that some features will end up being unfeasible due to time/browser constraints (especially the drawing and timelapse features due to their technical complexities). Our last resort fallback for the drawing editor is to require users to upload their art created in an external program. Our fallback for the timelapse feature is to push it to another checkpoint or drop it altogether because it isn’t critical to our design philosophy. | Drawing editor with TimeLapsing: Aden, CoverageTracking and StreakTracking (profile features): Julia, ReferencePhotoBinning and back-end photo selection: Eric, Posting and browsing art: Amina, Overall website and user flow: All of us |
| Usability checkpoint | At this stage, we hope to have a usable drawing editor and website layout. We still won’t be focused on the details of the interface (e.g. font, aesthetic pleasantness), but we want the editor to be acceptably stable and easy to use for most digital artists. This includes pen and touch support. If the drawing editor becomes unfeasible to implement entirely from scratch, our fallback plan is to rely on javascript APIs that implement browser drawing features already. | Drawing editor: Eric, Posting and browsing art: Amina, Profile page: Aden, Dashboard: Julia, Overall site usability: All of us |
| Finished product | Our final step will be to polish the app visually, fix usability issues, and address feedback from our peers. The ultimate goal of this step is to have a product that we are ready to present and submit. This includes a completed color and typography scheme. Our hope is that our planning will leave little to no risks at this stage. We won’t be making any major design revisions or code changes. | No particular assignments. We will use a ticket-like system where each teammate focuses on a particular issue (e.g. a bug or part of the interface to polish), fixes the issue in a branch, then merges it. |
